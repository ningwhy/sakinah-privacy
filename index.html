Sakinah — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Effective immediately

Sakinah ("we", "our", or "the App") is a Muslim companion application providing prayer times, Qibla direction, Quran reading, Dua, Tasbih, and related Islamic tools. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Core Principle: Local-First

Sakinah is designed with a local-first, privacy-first philosophy.

  • No account, no login, no sign-up required.
  • All your data — prayer logs, Tasbih counts, Quran bookmarks, cycle tracking, Zakat calculations — is stored entirely on your device using local databases (Isar / SharedPreferences).
  • We do not upload your personal data to any server.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Location Data (Device-Only)

Sakinah requests your device location only when the app is in use to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. Location data is processed on-device and is never transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Anonymous Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymous usage patterns (e.g., which features are most used, app crashes). Firebase collects an anonymous installation ID and device model information. We do NOT use IDFA or track you across other apps or websites. Firebase Analytics data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

2.3 Push Notification Token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

To deliver push notifications (e.g., Ramadan reminders, Eid greetings), we register a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token on your device. This token is a random identifier linked to your device, not to you personally.

2.4 Data You Enter

Prayer check-in records, Tasbih counts, Quran reading progress, Hifz tracking, cycle/wellness records, Zakat calculations, and sticker achievements — all stored locally on your device only. Clearing app data or uninstalling permanently deletes this data.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal identity: We don't ask for your name, email, phone, or address.
  • No account: No login, no registration, no password.
  • No advertising ID: IDFA is disabled in our Firebase configuration.
  • No cross-app tracking: We cannot and do not track you outside Sakinah.
  • No data selling: We never sell or share data with third parties.
  • No camera/microphone access for data collection: Microphone permission is only requested by a dependent library and Sakinah does not use it directly.

5. In-App Purchases

Sakinah VIP subscription is processed by Apple App Store / Google Play Billing. We do not handle or store any payment information. Subscription management is handled entirely by the platform stores.

6. Children's Privacy

Sakinah includes a child mode with age-appropriate content (stickers, simplified Tasbih, Asma learning). The App does not collect any personal information from children. No behavioral advertising. No user-generated content. No external links in child mode.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

All user-entered data is stored only on your device. To permanently delete all data:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Sakinah → Clear Data, or uninstall.
  • iOS: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Sakinah → Delete App.
  • Within the app: Wellness page → "Clear all data" option permanently erases health records.

8. Permissions Used

  • Location (When In Use): Prayer times & Qibla direction.
  • Notifications: Prayer reminders, Tasbih reminders, Zakat reminders.
  • Exact Alarm: Accurate Adhan alarm timing (Android).
  • Biometric (Face ID/Touch ID): Optional privacy lock for wellness data.
  • Background Fetch: Schedule local notifications.
  • Vibration: Notification vibration (Android).

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store your personal data on our servers, most privacy rights (GDPR, CCPA) are inherently satisfied. You have full control through your device settings. To opt out of Firebase Analytics, you can disable "Usage Data" in the app settings or reset the advertising identifier on your device.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, contact: privacy@sakinah.app

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.
